# 60-Second Video Demo Script: Scope-Creep Guard

**Goal:** Show how a freelancer goes from an awkward client request to an extra ₹18,000 billable invoice in under 60 seconds without leaking a single byte of client data.

---

### [00:00 - 00:10] The Hook
- **Visual:** Open Scope-Creep Guard in dark mode. Point cursor at the top badge: *"Runs 100% on your laptop. Your contract never leaves this device."*
- **Voiceover:** *"Clients always ask for 'just one quick change.' But saying 'no' is hard, and freelancers lose thousands in free labor. Meet Scope-Creep Guard — a local-first AI assistant powered by Google Gemma and Ollama that defends your contracts with 100% privacy."*

---

### [00:10 - 00:25] Scenario 1: The Subtle Scope Creep (Hinglish)
- **Visual:** Click **"Load Full Sample"**. The 3-page website contract loads into the left panel.
- **Visual:** Select the Hinglish scenario: *"Bhai ek chhota sa quick change tha — site pe Razorpay integrate kar do aur 25 products add kar do. Aur SEO bhi kar dena!"*
- **Visual:** Hit **"Analyze Scope with Gemma"**.
- **Voiceover:** *"Here's a realistic sample contract for a 3-page website. Our client sends a casual Hinglish message asking for payment gateway integration, product listings, and Google SEO ranking."*

---

### [00:25 - 00:40] The Instant AI Verdict & Financials
- **Visual:** Result pops up:
  - Red Badge: **OUT OF SCOPE**
  - Matched Clause: *“E-commerce features, product catalogs, and payment gateway integrations are strictly excluded.”*
  - Extra Charge: **18 billable hours · ₹18,000 extra charge**
- **Voiceover:** *"Instantly, Gemma detects the exact exclusion clause from the contract and calculates the financial impact: 18 extra hours, or ₹18,000 in billable revenue."*

---

### [00:40 - 00:50] 1-Click Diplomatic Replies & Mini Quote
- **Visual:** Click the 3 reply tabs: **Polite**, **Firm**, and **Friendly Upsell**.
- **Visual:** Click **"Copy Quote"** on the generated Change-Order Mini Quote.
- **Voiceover:** *"Instead of stressing over how to respond, you get 3 instant reply options — polite, firm, or a friendly upsell — plus a 1-click Change-Order Mini Quote ready to paste into WhatsApp."*

---

### [00:50 - 01:00] The Scope Ledger & Money Recovered
- **Visual:** Click over to the **Scope Ledger** tab.
- **Visual:** Toggle **"Mark as Agreed"**. Watch the **"Money Recovered"** card climb to ₹18,000 with a green glow.
- **Voiceover:** *"In the Scope Ledger, track every caught request. When your client agrees to pay, mark it recovered. Scope-Creep Guard: keeping your contracts tight, your clients happy, and your wallet full. 100% open-source and local."*
